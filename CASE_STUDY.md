# Koromosho Springs — case-study draft

## Classification

Personal concept project. Not client work and not a representation of a currently operating water company.

## Challenge

Turn a generic bottled-water landing page into a credible portfolio piece with a specific Kenyan point of view, while removing unverified business claims, testimonials, contact details, and fake form success states.

## Nash's role

Design direction and front-end development. Confirm before publishing any more specific role or timeline claim.

## Solution

- Reframed the experience around Eldoret homes, biashara, offices, and events.
- Built a restrained palette from Rift Valley green, mineral mist, terracotta, and cream.
- Replaced stock imagery with an original project-bound hero asset.
- Added clear sample product pricing and concept delivery zones.
- Replaced the fake order submission with a transparent estimator and copyable demo brief.
- Added responsive navigation, semantic structure, visible focus, reduced-motion handling, localized currency formatting, metadata, and CreativeWork structured data.

## Technology

HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript.

## Verified features

- Page loaded successfully over a local HTTP server.
- Responsive layouts passed at 320, 375, 768, 1024, and 1440 px with no horizontal overflow.
- Mobile navigation opened correctly and updated its `aria-expanded` state.
- The sample estimator returned the expected `KSh 1,000` total for three 20 L refills plus the inner-Eldoret concept delivery fee.
- The copyable order brief confirmed that no order was submitted.
- Browser checks reported no console or page errors.
