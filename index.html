The idea

A water brand that feels rooted, not imported.

Koromosho Springs imagines a calmer way to keep water stocked—built around the pace, language and practical realities of Eldoret life.

The visual system takes cues from Rift Valley greens, wet stone, rich soil and woven texture. The product experience keeps things direct: choose a size, see the delivery zone, understand the sample total.
