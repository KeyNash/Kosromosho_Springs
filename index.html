About Us

Fresh from the Springs of Eldoret

We believe everyone deserves access to clean, safe drinking water. That's why we go the extra mile to purify, package, and deliver the best water in the Rift Valley.

Why Koromosho Springs?

Located in the heart of Eldoret, we source our water from natural springs and put it through a rigorous 7-stage purification process. From filtration to UV treatment and ozonation, every drop is guaranteed safe and refreshing.

Whether you need a single bottle for your home or a bulk order for your office, we've got you covered with reliable delivery and friendly service.
